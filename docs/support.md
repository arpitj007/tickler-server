# Support

## Ask a question or share an idea

The best place is **GitHub Discussions**:

[github.com/arpitj007/tickler-server/discussions](https://github.com/arpitj007/tickler-server/discussions)

- **Q&A:** setup questions, "how do I...".
- **Ideas:** feature requests.
- **Show and tell:** how you use Tickler with your agents.
- **Beta feedback:** for TestFlight and Play testing builds.

Search first; someone may have had the same question.

## Report a bug

Open an issue: [github.com/arpitj007/tickler-server/issues](https://github.com/arpitj007/tickler-server/issues). Include:

- how you host (Cloudflare or Docker) and the server version (shown on `/api/health`)
- the app and phone (for example "iPhone app 1.0 on iOS 26")
- what you did, what you expected, what happened
- for server problems, the relevant log lines (on Docker: `docker logs tickler`)

**Never paste tokens, passphrases or provider keys.** They start with `tkd_`, `tkm_` or `tko_`, or are your providers' keys. If you posted one by accident, revoke it on `/setup` right away.

## Security issues

Please don't post them publicly. Email **security@gettickler.com**. See [Security](/security#reporting-a-problem).

## Email

support@gettickler.com, for things that don't fit in public. Replies may take a few days; it's a small project.
